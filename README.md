## The entry

One voicemail greeting, recorded by Miso the cat — caught mid-crime, knocking a mug off the table, too busy for your call:

> You've reached Miso. I'm busy knocking your mug off the table. Leave a message after the beep, or just bring tuna.

114 characters, plain text.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the greeting to `/output/entry.txt` — one line, no trailing newline — for the house judge to read.
